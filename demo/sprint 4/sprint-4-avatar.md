# Sprint 4 avatar demo

[sprint-4-avatar.mp4](sprint-4-avatar.mp4) has no audio. This note is the description of what the recording shows.

The demo follows the multiplayer workflow. One player creates a game. The other player joins that game with the room code. Once both players are in, each side sees the opponent's avatar across the board.

The avatar tracks where the opponent moves their head and their hand, which follows the controller. When the opponent teleports, the avatar moves from one place to another.
